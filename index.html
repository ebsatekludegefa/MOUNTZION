A Divine Call to Raise A Generation of Power and Faith

Mount Tsion The City of the Living God International Church was founded under the heavenly commission entrusted to Apostle Yidnekachew Shimels. Driven by a deep passion for prayer, deliverance, and sound biblical doctrine, the ministry has transformed thousands of lives across Ethiopia and around the world.

Headquartered in Bishoftu, Mount Tsion serves as a beacon of revival, equipped with apostolic grace, healing services, dynamic youth empowerment, and broadcast outreach through Voice of God TV.

Our Vision

To raise a transformed generation grounded in apostolic truth, unleashing the miraculous power of God, and expanding the Kingdom of the Living God across Ethiopia and every corner of the earth.

Our Mission

To preach the uncompromised Gospel through media, local assemblies, and global Zoom fellowship; equipping believers with sound doctrine, fervent prayer, worship, and active charity outreach.

Our 10-Year Path of Faith & Deliverance

From 2009 E.C. to 2019 E.C. - Reflecting on the trials, triumphs, and yearly Rhema Words that sustained Mount Tsion Church.

Overcoming Severe Losses & Trusting the Lord

The road of Mount Tsion Church has been marked by deep sacrifices. In the midst of our journey, Apostle Yidnekachew faced the profound loss of his beloved wife. Yet, like Job and Apostle Paul, his heart remained steadfast in God. Instead of backing down, his grief transformed into an overwhelming sanctuary of divine prayer, worship, and continuous devotion. Through this profound trial, the church learned the depth of trusting God in all circumstances, emerging stronger as a 10th-year victorious fellowship!
